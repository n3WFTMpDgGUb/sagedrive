Dad's Collection of Photos and Slides

In the late 1990's, Dad scanned a large collection of photos and slides to a CD and distributed it to the family. I've put the contents here for easy browsing. It's organized as two Galleries, one for photos and one for slides. Each Gallery has a Notes page composed of relevant text files from the CD.
