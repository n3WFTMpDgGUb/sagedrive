The MJS College Football Standings rank FBS teams in order of rating.  A
team's rating is computed as the sum of its winning percentage plus its
strength of schedule (SOS).  A team's SOS is computed as:

    SOS = 0.7 * [(average rating of opponents) - 0.5] 

A positive SOS means a team's schedule is stronger than average.  A
negative SOS means it is weaker.

Non-FBS opponents of FBS teams are treated as a single team "x" and a
rating computed for it in the same way.
