The MJS College Football Standings rank Division 1A teams in order of
rating.  A team's rating is computed as the sum of its winning
percentage plus its strength of schedule (SOS).  A team's SOS is
computed as:

    SOS = 0.7 * [(average rating of opponents) - 0.5] 

A positive SOS means a team's schedule is stronger than average.  A
negative SOS means it is weaker.

Non-Division-1A opponents of Division-1A teams are treated as a single
team "x" and a rating computed for it in the same way.
