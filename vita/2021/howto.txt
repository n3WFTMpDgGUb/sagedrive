CALIFORNIA HEALTH COVERAGE SHARED RESPONSIBILITY PAYMENT

Tax year 2021


Note:  These are instructions for a single TP with no dependents and
with no offer of employer coverage (i.e., Marketplace eligible). Needs
more thinking when there are more than one in tax family or if there is
offer of employer coverage; see instructions for Form 3853.

Go to CA Form 3853 Healthcare (shared responsibility payment).  Answer yes
or no to the question about coverage all 12 months.  If yes (coverage
all 12 months), or if there is no coverage and no exemption for each of
the 12 months, or if TP has no filing requirement, nothing more to do.
Otherwise as follows.

Check "no" for minimum essential healthcare.  Then do things in opposite
order to how they are listed by Taxslayer, as follows.

(1) Affordability exemption is not possible if eligible for CoveredCA
or MediCal.  Nevertheless, here's how to go through the exercise.

	Select Coverage Affordability Worksheet, answer "yes", enter the
	two premiums for LCBP and SLCSP.  Find monthly premiums for LCBP
	and SLCSP at coveredca.com; use it's search button for "3853",
	you'll find instructions.

		Remark: If coveredca.com shows TP is eligible
		for Medi-Cal, use 0 for SLCSP.	Likely results in
		no affordability exemption.

	Exit CA return and view PDF of return.	Find "Marketplace
	Coverage Affordability Worksheet" at or near end of return.
	Bottom line is annualized required contribution; write it down.
	Verify correctness of the worksheet, in particular, parameters
	of LCBP and SLCSP.

	Find "Affordability Worksheet" at or near end of return.
	Compare the number for "(A) Affordability Threshold" to the
	number you wrote down.	If the written down number is less than
	(A), not eligible for affordability exemption.

(2) Select Shared Responsibilty Payment.  Start a shared responsibility
payment worksheet for the individual.  Tag each month as indicated.

	Hint:  "No" means you have determined there is penalty for month;
	else "yes".  You need to manually determine this; Taxslayer
	does not figure it out for you.  These yes/no entries are what
	Taxslayer uses to calculate the SRP; all other entries related
	to exemptions are ignored.

If claiming affordability exemption, enter the annualized required
contribution (that you wrote down above) for each relevant month.
This will show up on the "Affordability Worksheet" of the return but is
otherwise ignored by Taxslayer.

(3) Select Coverage Exemptions and follow the dialog.  There needs to be
either an entry for full-year or entries for every month, and they need
to gibe with the yes/no answers you entered in (2).  This will show up
on the "Coverage and Exemption Codes" page of the return but is otherwise
ignored by Taxslayer.

	Hint:  The entry "Select" in the dropdown box is the correct
	choice for no coverage / no exemption.

(4) Exit CA return, view PDF, and verify correctness of of the health
coverage forms.
