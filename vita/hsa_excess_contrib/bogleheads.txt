EXCESS EMPLOYER HSA CONTRIBUTION -- EARNED OR UNEARNED INCOME?

A friend had excess employer contribution to HSA of $1500 in 2022.
He requested removal, it was done, and he has 1099-SA showing distribution
of $1600: $1500 excess contribution, $100 earnings on excess contribution.
Turbotax enters $1600 of additional income on 1040 Schedule 1, Line 8f,
"income from form 8889."

The amount is clearly correct. What doesn't seem right is that Turbotax
treats the entire amount as unearned income.  Seems to me that $1500
should be earned income, since it was excluded from W2 salary (box 1).
As earned income, it would be subject to self-employment tax, which
also seems right because it would have been subject to payroll tax had
it been included in W2 salary.

However, I don't find anything in Pub 969 or Form 8889 instructions
that discusses earned versus unearned income.  Can anybody point to a
reference that resolves whether it's earned or unearned?
