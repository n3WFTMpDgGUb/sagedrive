SCENARIO

Tax year 2022.

TP (age 60) was HSA-eligible for 8 months (contribution limit $3,100).
At TP's request, employer made full-year's HSA contribution (W2 box 12
code W $4,650).  Result is excess contribution of 4650 - 3100 = $1,550.

TP did not request removal of excess contribution.

TP requested and has 1099-SA showing showing normal distribution (box 3
code 1) of $3200.  TP has qualifying medical expenses greater than $3200.

TP has records showing HSA earned $200 interest in 2022.


BACKGROUND

4012 (page E-8) says "If the excess contributions and earnings are not
withdrawn by the due date of the return, including extensions, then the
return is out of scope."

(See fidelity_hsa_excess.pdf for example of how to request removal.)


SOLUTION

One option is to tell TP it's OOS until he returns with 1099-SA
documenting excess-contribution removal (box 3 code 2).  Reasonable,
because it's difficult to accurately calculate earnings on excess
contribution.  However, earnings are likely small, hate to turn TP away
for it.

Better option is to use TP records to determine approximate earnings
on excess contribution, say $150.  Split his 1099-SA into two, one for
excess contribution removal [$1700 box 1, $150 box 2, box 3 code 2) and
one with normal distribution [$1500 box 1, box 3 code 1].  Results in
additional taxable income of $1,700 Fed, $0 CA.


TURBOTAX ENTRY

Enter the two 1099-SAs as in the worksheets in taxlayer_hsa.pdf.
Results in $1700 of "other income" on 1040 Schedule 1, which is backed
out for CA on 540-CA Section B; both are in rows labeled "income from
form 8889."  Note that TT treats entire amount as unearned income; seems
to me should be $1550 earned, $150 unearned, but absent authoritative
reference will go with TT.  Enter the $200 HSA interest as usual, shows
up as addition on 540-CA.


TAXSLAYER ENTRY

Enter the three numbers ($3200, $1700, $1500) into 1099-SA dialog, per
the screenshot in taxslayer_hsa.pdf.  This results in correctly filled
out Form 8889, as shown in the return (also in the pdf).

However, Taxslayer does not handle the income adjustment automatically;
it requires manual entries, as follows:

- Needs manual entry of $1700 as "Other income".  Per TT, call it
unearned and label it "Income from Form 8889".  Appears on a different
row of Schedule 1 than it does with TT but result is same.

- Need to back it out for CA as "Subtraction -> Other Income".  TS does
not allow digits in label and limits to 20 characters, so I settled on
"From Fed HSA Form"; explanatory note makes it clear it offsets "Income
from form 8889".  Appears on a different row of 540-CA than it does with
TT but result is same.

- For HSA interest, TS does not seem to work for CA-540 "Addition ->
Interest". Instead use "Addition -> Other Income" and label it "HSA
Interest."  Appears on Section B of 540-CA rather than Section A as with
TT, but result is the same.
