Note: A fiscal year begins on July 1 and ends on June 30. The valuation date for a year is June 30, i.e., the close of the fiscal year. For example, for the entries for the year 2000, the valuation date is 30 June 2000, and the fiscal year is 1 July 1999 to 30 June 2000.

  1. Accrued liability is the total liability for benefits already earned for past service as reported by CalPERS on the valuation date.
  2. Actual value of assets is the market value of investments as reported by CalPERS on the valuation date.
  3. Actual unfunded liability is [1] − [2].
  4. Actual funded status is [2] ÷ [1].
  5. Actual investment return is the investment return for the fiscal year that ends on the valuation date, as reported by CalPERS.
  6. Net inflow is [2] ÷ sqrt(1 +[5]) − [2, preceding year] × sqrt(1 +[5]).
  7. Value of assets, X% return for 1993 is [2]. For other years, it is [7, preceding year] × (1 + X) + [6] × sqrt(1 + X).
  8. Unfunded liability, X% return is [1] − [7].
  9. Funded status, X% return is [7] ÷ [1].

Data sources:

  • Accrued liability is from [CAFR2003, p. 90], [CAFR2013, p. 132], and [CAFR2019, p. 122].
  • Actual value of assets is the market value of assets from [CAFR2007, p. 106], [CAFR2013, p. 132], and [CAFR2019, p. 122] for 1997-2018. For 1993-1996, the CAFRs do not report the market value of assets; for these years, the funded status was obtained by measuring the plot in [RISK2014, p. 4], and the market value of assets was computed by multiplying the accrued liability by the measured funded status.
  • Actual investment return is from [VAL2013, p. 20] and [VAL2018, p. 13].
